Officer,
 
and
 
Admin

via
 
ASP.NET
 
Core
 
Identity

 •  All  users  authenticate  via  email/password;  Admin  can  also  create  internal  system  accounts  
directly.
 •  All  users  can  update  their  profile,  contact  information,  and  password.  •  In-app  and  email  alerts  are  dispatched  for  low-stock,  overstock,  pending  PO  approvals,  and  
overdue
 
receipts
 
using
 
SendGrid
 
SDK.
 •  Full  audit  trail:  every  stock  movement,  PO  status  change,  and  user  action  is  logged  with  
timestamp
 
and

using
 
EF
 
Core

 •  Multi-warehouse  support:  stock  levels  are  tracked  independently  per  warehouse  with

EF
 
Core
 
transactions.
  
2.2  Warehouse  Staff  Requirements  •  View  real-time  stock  levels  for  all  products  across  assigned  warehouses.  •  Record  stock  receipts  (Goods  Received  Notes)  against  approved  Purchase  Orders.  •  Record  stock  issues  /  consumption  for  production,  sales,  or  internal  use.  •  Perform  manual  stock  adjustments  (corrections,  write-offs,  damaged  goods).  •  Transfer  stock  between  warehouse  locations  with  reason  codes.  •  Scan  product  barcodes  or  QR  codes  using  ZXing.Net  to  look  up  product  details  and  current  
stock.
 •  Conduct  cycle  count  /  stock  audit  and  record  discrepancies.  •  View  stock  movement  history  for  any  product  and  warehouse.  •  Acknowledge  and  dismiss  low-stock  or  overstock  alerts.   
2.3  Inventory  Manager  Requirements  •  Create,  edit,  and  deactivate  products  with  full  metadata:  SKU,  barcode,  category,  brand,  unit  of  
measure,
 
cost
 
price,
 
selling
 
price,
 
and
 
images.
 •  Define  reorder  level,  maximum  stock  level,  and  lead  time  per  product.  •  Search  and  filter  products  by  name,  category,  brand,  SKU,  or  barcode.  •  View  real-time  stock  levels  across  all  warehouses  with  drill-down  to  location  detail.  •  View  and  configure  warehouse  facilities:  capacity,  manager  assignment,  and  activation  status.  •  View  complete  stock  movement  audit  trail  with  filters  by  date,  type,  product,  and  warehouse.  •  Access  inventory  analytics:  total  stock  valuation,  inventory  turnover  rate,  top-moving  products,  
slow-moving
 
products,
 
and
 
dead
 
stock.
 •  Generate  and  export  inventory  reports  (daily  snapshot,  valuation  summary,  movement  
summary)
 
via
 
EPPlus
 
/
 
iTextSharp.
 •  Configure  automatic  low-stock  alert  thresholds  per  product.  •  Initiate  stock  transfers  between  warehouses.
